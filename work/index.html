Slide decks, memos, writing and video from my past work. Every link you open sends more renewable power into the carbon block above, and the block sends heat out. Open twelve and the core reaches 2,400 °C, the same ceiling as Antora's carbon thermal battery.

Core temperature follows the glow of heated carbon, from dull red near 700 °C to white at 2,400 °C. Heat output tops out at 375 °C, the temperature of Antora's HeatCore. All values rise with the number of links opened and are illustrations.
